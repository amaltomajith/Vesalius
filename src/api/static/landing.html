Open Vesalius How it works

It starts with a scan.

A CT arrives as a stack of DICOM slices, the format every hospital scanner already writes. Vesalius reads them exactly as they come.

Private by construction.

Names, IDs, dates and the face are stripped before anything is rebuilt. The slices are then reassembled into one volume: the anatomy survives, the identity does not.

AI names every structure.

A segmentation model that knows 117 anatomical structures labels every bone, organ and vessel it finds, in place, in 3D.

Learning that from flat textbook plates takes months. Here you walk around it.

Steer it with your hands.

Your webcam tracks 21 points on each hand. Pinch and drag to turn, point at a part and hold to select it, sweep your other hand to clear the rest away. The video never leaves your browser.
